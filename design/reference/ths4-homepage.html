The five dimensions

One score, and the reason behind it.

A number nobody can audit is worth nothing. Every dimension resolves to named on-chain checks, carries a fixed weight, and reports the block it was read at.

Security

30Weight
  • Honeypot simulated on a live router
  • Mint authority in storage
  • Blacklist and pause functions
  • Proxy resolved to implementation
  • Source verification

Liquidity

25Weight
  • Pool depth
  • LP lock share and unlock date
  • Routing concentration
  • Realised slippage on exit

Tokenomics

20Weight
  • Top-10 holder concentration
  • Unlock schedule
  • Team allocation
  • Transfer tax, buy and sell

Community

15Weight
  • Holder growth
  • Wallet retention
  • Supply held over 90 days

Development

10Weight
  • Contract activity
  • Upgrade cadence
  • Deployer history

What a scan cannot tell you. A clean score means the checks above passed at a specific block. It cannot see off-chain intent, a team that rugs a contract it fully controls, or a governance vote that has not happened yet. Anyone selling you certainty about a token is selling you something else.

Methodology

Before you paste an address.

What does the health score actually mean?
A 0-100 number built from five dimensions: security, liquidity, tokenomics, community and development. Each resolves to specific on-chain checks, and the weights are identical on every scan so two tokens compare directly.
Does a high score mean a token is safe?
No. It means the checks that ran passed at a specific block. It cannot account for off-chain intent, a team acting on a contract it controls, or a future governance change. Treat it as evidence, not a guarantee.
Do I have to connect my wallet?
No. Token Health Scan reads public on-chain data about a contract address. It never needs access to your wallet, and there is nothing to approve or sign.
What do I get for free, and what costs money?
Three levels. With no account you get the overall score and all five category scores. A free account unlocks the per-check detail underneath, and includes 3 lifetime Pro Scans. Pro is $20 a month for 10 Pro Scans, or $120 a year.
What is the AI Agent Trust Score?
A separate scan for ERC-8004 agents. It resolves the agent's registry record and onchain identity into a 0-100 trust score, so you can check an agent before delegating funds or signing authority to it. Three agent scans are free. Results cache for six hours.
Which chains are supported?
Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon and Solana.
